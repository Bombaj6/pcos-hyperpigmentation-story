# PCOS Hyperpigmentation Story-Mode Landing Page

An interactive, high-converting story-mode landing page designed for Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) hyperpigmentation and Acanthosis Nigricans education & product conversion (inspired by the Strawberry skincare formulation).

## 📖 Key Story Angles & Features
- **Editorial Hook ("The Ponytail Rule"):** Focuses on the real emotional and daily struggles of women living with PCOS-related skin darkening on the neck, underarms, and inner thighs.
- **Root Cause Education:** Explains insulin resistance, hyperinsulinemia, elevated androgens, and why abrasive scrubbing worsens Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH).
- **The 5-Ingredient Clinical Complex:**
  - 6% Glycolic Acid (AHA)
  - 2% Tranexamic Acid
  - 2% Kojic Acid Dipalmitate
  - 2% Alpha-Arbutin
  - 5% Niacinamide
- **Interactive Before & After Area Viewer:** Interactive tab selector comparing results on Neck, Underarms, and Inner Thighs.
- **Realistic Timeline Progression:** Clear week-by-week milestones (Weeks 1-2, 3-4, 5-8).
- **Social Proof & Verified Customer Reviews:** 5-star testimonials with verified badges.
- **Package Selection & Risk-Free Offer:** Interactive package selector with 60-day satisfaction guarantee and sticky mobile CTA bar.
